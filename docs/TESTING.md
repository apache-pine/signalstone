# Manual live-Webex checklist

## Status as of 1.0

Every feature area below has been exercised against live Webex during
development and confirmed working — auth, messaging, threads, markdown,
mentions, realtime (including all five issues under "Realtime" in
`docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`), notifications, read receipts, space
management, avatars/presence, the conversation-list context menu
(favorites, hide/unhide), and the image lightbox all specifically got a
"works" confirmation at least once. This checklist isn't a one-time gate
that gets checked off and archived, though — it stays here as the reference
for exercising the same surface again after a future change, so the boxes
below are deliberately left unchecked rather than retroactively marked from
memory of exactly which line was tested when.

Two items have *not* been exercised against the real thing yet, flagged
individually below and in "Pending live tests" at the bottom: SDK
reconnect/recovery after a genuine network interruption (the logic and its
unit tests exist; only a live network drop hasn't been tried), and the
Adaptive Card fallback against an actual card-sending bot.

## Troubleshooting realtime/notification delivery

If messages aren't appearing live or notifications seem inconsistent, turn on **Debug logging** in Signalstone's settings (Advanced section), reproduce the issue, then open DevTools (Ctrl+Shift+I / Cmd+Option+I) → Console and filter for `Signalstone:`. Every `[Signalstone:sdk]` line traces an event from the SDK; `[Signalstone:store]` traces how Signalstone routed it (open conversation vs. background vs. notify); `[Signalstone:main]` traces the final notification decision. Nothing logged means the event never reached that stage — that narrows down where to look far faster than guessing. Never logs your token or message content. Turn it back off when done; it's off by default.

- [ ] Install in a dedicated vault; enable, disable, re-enable, and reload.
- [ ] Open Settings → Signalstone: confirm Connection/Composing/Notifications/Appearance/Conversations/Advanced/About render as grouped sections (not a flat list), the token field still works (select/change a secret, connection rebuilds), "Test connection" and "Disconnect" update the status text without a full settings reopen, and every dropdown/toggle below persists across an Obsidian restart. This is the declarative settings API (Obsidian 1.13.0+); it's new territory worth confirming directly since it can't be unit-tested (the `obsidian` package ships no runtime implementation to test against).
- [ ] **Settings behavior, one at a time** (each should apply live, without needing to reopen the view or reconnect, except where noted):
  - [ ] "Send message with": switch to Shift+Enter; confirm plain Enter now inserts a newline and Shift+Enter sends. Switch back.
  - [ ] "Notify me for" → "Direct messages and @mentions": from another client, `@mention` yourself in a group space you don't have open — confirm a notification fires; send an unrelated message into that same space without mentioning you — confirm it does *not* notify; send a direct message — confirm it always notifies.
  - [ ] "Show a message preview": turn off; confirm a notification shows only who it's from, no text snippet.
  - [ ] "Message spacing" → Compact: confirm the message list visibly tightens. Switch back to Comfortable.
  - [ ] "Time format": switch between Match system / 12-hour / 24-hour; confirm message timestamps and the conversation list's timestamps update accordingly.
  - [ ] "Sort conversations by" → Alphabetical: confirm the conversation list reorders by title instead of recent activity. Switch back to Most recent activity.
  - [ ] "Confirm before deleting messages": turn off; confirm clicking Delete on your own message deletes it immediately, with no "Confirm delete" step.
  - [ ] "Automatically load attachments": turn on; open a conversation with an image/file attachment and confirm it loads without a click. Turn back off and confirm the click-to-load button returns.
  - [ ] "Always scroll to the newest message": turn off; scroll up to read history in an active conversation, then have another client send a new message; confirm the view does *not* jump to the bottom. Send/receive while already scrolled to the bottom; confirm it still auto-scrolls.
  - [ ] "Sidebar side": switch to Left, close the Signalstone view entirely, then reopen it (ribbon icon or command); confirm it opens in the left sidebar. Switch back to Right and repeat.
  - [ ] "Open Signalstone when Obsidian starts": turn on, fully restart Obsidian; confirm the view opens automatically without clicking anything.
  - [ ] "Realtime polling frequency": with the connection intentionally degraded (see the Polling test below), switch between Frequent/Normal/Relaxed and confirm the interval visibly changes (e.g. via Debug logging timestamps) without needing to reconnect.
  - [ ] "Messages per page": switch to 25, open a space with more than 25 messages, and confirm "Load older messages" now returns roughly 25 at a time instead of 50.
  - [ ] "Track unread messages": turn off; from another client, send into a space you don't have open, then confirm nothing marks it unread anywhere (no badge, no divider, no ribbon count) and turning it off also visually hides the five settings below it. Turn back on.
- [ ] With no token configured, open the Signalstone view and click "Open settings": confirm Obsidian's Settings modal actually opens, on Signalstone's own tab (not just a Notice telling you where to go). If `app.setting` is ever missing (see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, "Opening Obsidian's own Settings modal"), confirm it falls back to the original Notice instead of doing nothing.
- [ ] Validate a good, invalid, and expired token.
- [ ] Load/filter recent direct and group spaces and older messages.
- [ ] Send a message using `**bold**`, `*italic*`, a `[link](url)`, a bulleted list with a nested sub-item, a `> blockquote`, and a fenced code block; confirm each renders correctly both in Signalstone and in another Webex client (proves the `markdown` field round-trips correctly both ways). Send a multi-line message (Shift+Enter between lines) and confirm each line stays on its own line in both places, not collapsed together. Receive a Markdown message and an `@mention` from another client; confirm formatting and the mention render (not raw `<@personEmail:...>` syntax).
- [ ] Send a message with a deliberate blank line in the middle (type a line, Shift+Enter twice, type another line): confirm the draft shows a full blank line while typing, and — this was a real bug, fixed but worth confirming directly — confirm the *sent* message still shows that same blank line once it comes back from the server, in both Signalstone and another Webex client, not collapsed together.
- [ ] Receive a multi-line message pasted straight from somewhere else (no deliberate Webex hard-break formatting) — NYT Wordle's own share text (paste it into another Webex client and send) is a good real-world example, one grid row per line. Confirm each row renders on its own line in Signalstone, not soft-joined into one long line that happens to wrap at the sidebar's current width.
- [ ] Confirm the header changes from `Connecting…` to `Live`; send from another client and verify it appears without waiting for the 15-second fallback poll.
- [ ] With a conversation *already open*, send a message into it from another client; confirm it appears immediately (not after a 15-second delay). This exact scenario was broken by two separate, now-fixed issues — see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md` — Realtime, issues 3 and 4 — so it's worth checking deliberately rather than assuming the general "receive a message" test above covers it.
- [ ] With no conversation open, have another client send into a space you're not viewing; confirm a background notification still fires (if notifications are enabled) — issue 4 broke this too, silently.
- [ ] With the connection solidly `Live` (not just momentarily), have another client send into a space that is *not* the currently selected one but *is* already visible in your conversation list; confirm that space jumps to the top of the list within a second or two, not after a ~45-second delay. This was issue 5 — the list previously only reordered on a `refresh-space-list` event, which stopped arriving reliably once live. Separately, send yourself a message into whichever conversation is currently open and confirm it also jumps to the top once you back out to the list.
- [ ] Temporarily block/reject the realtime connection; confirm the header shows `Polling`, messages still refresh, and `Live` returns after recovery.
- [ ] If the header stays on `Polling`, hover it for the detail text; if messages don't appear live despite the header showing `Live`, turn on Debug logging (see above) first. Five known issues (a `setDeviceInfo` crash, a confirmed CORS block, a room-id encoding mismatch, a missing `@webex/plugin-people` registration, and a conversation list that went stale once live) were found and fixed — see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md` — Realtime. This checklist item is to catch a *new* failure, not one of those five. If it's a new message, open DevTools (Ctrl+Shift+I / Cmd+Option+I) → Console and Network tabs — an uncaught error naming a specific SDK file/function (as issue 4's did) is usually traceable the same way; a `wss://` (WebSocket) connection attempt that's rejected or closed points at the Mercury transport specifically.
- [ ] Upload PNG, JPEG, GIF, PDF, and a document; paste and drag a file.
- [ ] Load an image attachment, then click it: confirm it opens full-size in a modal (not the small inline preview), with Escape, clicking the backdrop, and clicking the image itself all closing it. Confirm a non-image file attachment (PDF/document) is unaffected — no click-to-enlarge, still just the filename/Save row.
- [ ] Load an image attachment (GIF and a regular image), then click "🙈 Unload" next to Save: confirm it reverts to the "📎 Load attachment" button, same as if it had never been loaded. Click "Load attachment" again and confirm it re-downloads and displays correctly (not stuck, not showing stale content). Confirm a non-image file attachment shows no Unload button at all. Load two images in the same conversation and unload only one; confirm the other stays loaded and unaffected. With "Automatically load attachments" on, load a conversation, unload one image, then leave and reopen the conversation; confirm auto-load re-fetches it again on the fresh mount (unloading only reverts that one instance, not a persistent preference).
- [ ] **Adaptive cards — needs a bot/integration that actually sends one** (e.g. a webhook test bot, or any existing Approval/poll bot in a space you're in): confirm the card's text content (titles, labels, fact values) renders instead of a blank message, action button titles appear as a plain informational line (not as real buttons), and there's a note that the card isn't interactive here. Confirm nothing crashes or renders raw JSON. If you don't have a card-sending bot handy, this can wait — it's the one item on this list not yet exercised against a real card.
- [ ] Cancel and confirm deletion of an own message.
- [ ] **Selecting and copying message text** (see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, "Selecting and copying message text" — this was previously reported as not working at all): click-and-drag across a single message's text, both a received message and your own; confirm it highlights like ordinary selectable text, and Ctrl/Cmd+C (or the browser's native right-click Copy on the highlighted text) copies exactly what was selected. Drag a selection starting in one message's body and ending in a later one, several messages down; confirm the whole range highlights continuously and copies together, spanning the messages in between too (unlike Webex's own client, which only allows one at a time).
- [ ] Right-click a message with nothing selected: confirm a "Copy message" menu item appears (not the OS/Electron default menu), and clicking it copies that message's full text — try this on both an own and a received message. Now highlight a portion of a message's text and right-click *on the highlighted text*: confirm the native context menu appears instead (with its own Copy), not Signalstone's "Copy message" item, and that copying it copies only the highlighted portion, not the whole message.
- [ ] Turn off "Allow selecting message text" in settings: confirm dragging across message text no longer highlights anything, in either an own or received message. Confirm right-click "Copy message" still works regardless (it doesn't depend on this setting). Turn it back on.
- [ ] Turn off "Include the sender name when selecting": drag a selection starting inside one message's body, through the sender-name/timestamp line above the *next* message, and into that next message's body; confirm the resulting selection skips the name/timestamp line entirely — the copied text should be just the two messages' bodies, with no name in between. Turn the setting back on and repeat the same drag; confirm the name/timestamp line is now included in the selection.
- [ ] In "New message" or "New space", search for a couple of real directory contacts and confirm their photo and a colored presence dot both appear next to their name (green/red/yellow depending on their current status; hover the dot for the label). Search for someone with no avatar set and confirm the row still renders cleanly (no broken-image icon).
- [ ] Turn on "Show presence in the conversation list" only (avatars off): confirm a colored dot appears next to each direct-message row's title, but no photo, and group-space rows show neither. Turn on "Show avatars in the conversation list" too and confirm the photo now also appears for direct messages. Confirm both settings apply within a few seconds of turning them on (the immediate refresh), not only after the next natural conversation-list refresh.
- [ ] Turn on both "…in direct message conversations" settings, open a direct message, and confirm the photo and a presence dot appear in the header next to the other person's name; confirm neither appears when opening a group space. Have that person change their status in another Webex client (e.g. join a meeting) and confirm the dot updates in Signalstone within roughly a polling interval — not instantly, since this is refresh-based, not live (see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, "Avatars and presence").
- [ ] With every avatar/presence setting off (the default), turn on Debug logging and confirm no People API calls related to avatars/presence occur on a normal conversation-list refresh — this should cost nothing until at least one setting is on.
- [ ] Right-click a group space in the list: confirm Open, Favorite/Unfavorite, Manage members, Rename…, and a red "Leave this space…" all appear, in that order (no Hide — confirmed live that Webex's server rejects it for a group membership). Click "Manage members" and confirm it opens directly into the member panel (not the normal message view). Go back, right-click again, click "Rename…", and confirm it opens directly into the inline title editor. Right-click again, click "Leave this space…", confirm a second small menu appears with Cancel/red-"Leave this space" rather than leaving immediately, click Cancel, and confirm nothing happened.
- [ ] Right-click a direct message in the list: confirm Open, Favorite/Unfavorite, Copy email address, and "Hide this conversation" all appear (no Manage members/Rename/Leave — those are group-only). Click "Copy email address" and paste somewhere to confirm it copied the correct address. Click "Hide this conversation": confirm a Notice appears, the conversation disappears from the list, and — critically — messages arriving in it afterward do *not* trigger a notification even with notifications enabled. Turn on "Show hidden conversations" in settings and confirm it reappears, dimmed and marked "Hidden". Right-click it there and confirm the menu now offers "Unhide this conversation" instead of "Hide"; click it, confirm a Notice appears, and turn "Show hidden conversations" back off to confirm it's gone again (this time for real, not just dimmed).
- [ ] Right-click a couple of conversations (mix of group and direct) and "Add to favorites" on each: confirm a ★ appears next to the title, and both jump to the top of the list, above everything else, regardless of the current "Sort conversations by" setting. Right-click one and confirm the menu now offers "Remove from favorites"; click it, confirm the star disappears and it returns to its normal sorted position. Fully restart Obsidian and confirm the remaining favorite is still favorited and still sorted first — this is local-only data (see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, "Favorites"), so it must survive a restart on its own, not by re-deriving anything from Webex.
- [ ] From the conversation list, click 👥 "Create a new space": enter a name, search for and add a couple of members (plus one deliberately-invalid email to confirm the "could not add" message surfaces without blocking space creation), and confirm the new space opens with those members present (check the member panel). Create a space with no members at all and confirm that also works.
- [ ] Create a new space (or start a new DM via ＋), and once it opens, click the ← back button: confirm it returns to the recent conversations list, not back to the "New space"/"New message" form (this was a real bug — the form used to reappear until this was fixed).
- [ ] In an open group space, click the ✎ rename icon in the header: rename it, confirm the new title shows immediately and persists after switching away and back. Confirm ✎ does *not* appear for a direct space or while a thread is focused.
- [ ] In a group space's member panel, click "Leave this space", cancel once, then confirm: verify you land back on the conversation list and the space is gone from it. Confirm the same button does *not* appear anywhere for a direct space.
- [ ] In a group space, open the member panel (👥): add a member by email, promote/demote a moderator, and cancel/confirm removing a member. Confirm a direct message shows no member panel.
- [ ] From another client, open a group space you also have open in Signalstone and read up to a specific message there (e.g. click into the conversation, or scroll to the bottom). Confirm "Seen by [that person]" appears under that exact message in Signalstone within a second or two — and, if they then read a later message, confirm the receipt moves to that later message instead. Confirm your own reading of messages in Signalstone does *not* produce a "Seen by you" anywhere (Signalstone never sends a read receipt of its own — see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, "Read/unread state"). Restart Obsidian and confirm no stale receipts survive from before the restart (this is live-only, nothing is persisted).
- [ ] In a group space's composer, type `@` and part of a member's name; confirm a filtered suggestion list appears (mouse click, and separately Arrow keys + Enter, to select), the composer shows friendly `@Name` text, and the sent message shows as a real mention to another Webex client (not raw `<@personEmail:...>` text). Type `@a` and confirm `@all` is offered; send it and confirm it mentions everyone. Confirm typing `@` in a direct space's composer does *not* open a suggestion list.
- [ ] Test offline/recovery without losing loaded content or draft.
- [ ] Test light/dark themes, narrow sidebar, keyboard focus, long URLs, and code.
- [ ] Confirm no history is persisted and no GIPHY integration exists.
- [ ] Set notifications to "All messages". From another client, send a message into a space that is *not* currently open in Signalstone; confirm a Notice appears with sender and a short preview. Send a message into the space that *is* open; confirm no Notice appears. Send a message yourself from Signalstone; confirm no self-notification. Switch to "Direct messages only" and confirm a background group-space message no longer notifies while a background DM still does. Switch to "Off" and confirm neither notifies.
- [ ] **No duplicate notifications/unread marks** (regression — see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, Realtime issue 6): with the connection solidly `Live`, send one message into a background space and confirm exactly one Notice appears and the badge count goes up by exactly one, not two. Open that conversation (clearing its unread state), then wait roughly 45+ seconds — long enough for the background space-list poll to run again — and confirm the badge does *not* reappear on its own with no new message having arrived.
- [ ] **Unread messages** (all four presentation settings on, the default — see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, "Unread messages"): from another client, send two or three messages into a space you don't have open. Confirm a number badge appears on that conversation's row in the list, distinguishable at a glance from the presence dot (different shape/position, not just color). Confirm the ribbon icon also shows a badge with the total unread count across all spaces. Open that conversation: confirm a "N new messages" divider appears above the first message you hadn't seen, and a jump-to-unread button is visible above the list; scroll away from the divider and confirm the button is still there and clicking it scrolls straight back to the divider. Scroll the divider itself into view, then scroll away again and confirm it does *not* disappear or move — it should stay put for the rest of this viewing session regardless of scroll position. Leave the conversation (back to Recents) and reopen it: confirm the badge/divider/button are now gone (read on open) even though you never scrolled the divider into view that first time. Send yourself more messages while the conversation is open and confirm they do *not* retroactively grow the divider's count. Have the sender delete one of the still-unread messages from another client; confirm the count drops accordingly without restarting.
- [ ] **Unread messages — settings independence**: with tracking on, turn each of the five presentation settings off one at a time (returning it to on before testing the next) and confirm the UI still looks correct with only that one off — no leftover empty badge/divider space, no broken layout, and (specifically for the divider setting) the jump button still works even with the visible divider text hidden. Then turn all five off together and confirm the conversation list, an open conversation, and the ribbon icon all look exactly as they did before this feature existed. Restart Obsidian with unread messages already pending in a background space and confirm everything starts fresh as read (no unread state survives a restart — this is session-local by design).
- [ ] **Mark as read, per conversation**: with a conversation open and its jump-to-unread button/divider showing, click the "Mark as read" button next to it; confirm a small confirmation menu appears first (not an immediate action), and that clicking outside it or its own Cancel item does nothing. Confirm it. Verify the divider, jump button, and this conversation's badge in the list all disappear immediately, without leaving and reopening the conversation. Confirm the conversation list badge and ribbon badge both decrease by exactly this conversation's unread count.
- [ ] **Mark all as read, header button**: with nothing unread, confirm a "✓" button is still visible in the conversation-list header but greyed out/disabled (this is deliberate — see `docs/WEBEX_CAPABILITIES.md`, "Unread messages" — its visibility is controlled only by the setting below, not by whether anything is currently unread). From another client, send into two or more conversations; confirm the button becomes enabled, and clicking it opens a confirmation menu naming the total count before doing anything. Confirm it and verify every badge in the list, every open conversation's divider/jump button, and the ribbon badge are all cleared at once, and the button goes back to disabled. Turn off "Show a 'mark all as read' button" in settings and confirm the button disappears entirely (not just disabled) even with unread conversations present; turn it back on and confirm it reappears immediately, in whatever enabled/disabled state matches the current unread count — this exact toggle was the regression this checklist item exists to catch.
- [ ] **Jump button auto-loads older pages when needed**: set "Messages per page" low (e.g. 25) in settings, open a group space, then from another client send more messages into it than that page size while it's *not* the open conversation (e.g. 30+). Reopen it and click the jump-to-unread button: confirm it briefly shows "Loading…" (older pages fetching automatically, since the earliest unread message wasn't in the initial page), then scrolls to the correct "N new messages" divider above the true first unread message — not one that was already loaded and merely happened to be the earliest of those. Restore "Messages per page" to your normal value afterward.
